Join the club See meeting times

Four ways we show up for our community.

Every meeting and event ties back to one of these pillars.

Disease prevention

Easy-to-start habits that keep us healthy, like fall-prevention basics for seniors and sun-safety habits for kids.

Mental wellness

Workshops on stress management, sleep health, and how physical and mental health shape each other.

Community outreach

Visiting senior living communities to talk about fall prevention, and sunscreen giveaways that teach kids about skin cancer.

Medical exploration

Guest talks from healthcare professionals, journal clubs on medical research, and guidance for the pre-med path.
